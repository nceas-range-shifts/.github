# Welcome to the NCEAS range shifts Github!

This is the Github home for the National Center for Ecological Analysis & Synthesis (NCEAS) led research in collaboration with the US Geological Survey (USGS) Climate Adaptation Postdoctoral (CAP) Fellows Program. The 2025-2027 CAP cohort is focused on investigating the future of species range shifts.  
### [More info on the USGS 2025-2027 Range Shift Cohort](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/climate-adaptation-science-centers/science/cap-future-species-range-shifts-cohort-2025)
